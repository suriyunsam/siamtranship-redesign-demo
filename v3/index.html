Our approach

You choose the fish. We handle the journey.

Live fish need export paperwork, the right packing, an airline slot and a courier waiting at the other end. We run that whole chain for one booking, so you are never coordinating four companies across two countries.

What's included

How it works

How a shipment runs.

Four steps, and you only take part in the first two. You get an email each time your fish move on.

  1. 01

    Buy

    Choose any breeder or shop in Thailand and pay them directly. We never sell fish.

  2. 02

    Book

    Your seller, your US address and a departure. Pay online by PayPal or card.

  3. 03

    Fly

    Collected, packed and documented in Bangkok, then on the consolidated flight.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Cleared in the US and handed to a courier for delivery to your door.
