How it works

Four stages: buy, book, fly, deliver.

  1. 01

    Buy

    Choose any breeder or shop in Thailand and pay them directly. We never sell fish.

  2. 02

    Book

    Tell us your seller, your US address and a departure, and pay the transhipping fee online.

  3. 03

    Fly

    We collect the fish in Bangkok, pack them in insulated export boxes, document them and put them on the flight.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Cleared in the US and handed to a domestic courier, with email updates at every stage.

Pricing

Your price, before you book.

Shipping depends on your state's zone and how many fish you send. Handling is $5 per fish, and your first two fish together are $5. The fish price is paid to your Thai seller.
